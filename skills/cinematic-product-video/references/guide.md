# Cinematic product video: decision guide

The self-contained [skill](../SKILL.md) holds the complete production workflow. This optional guide records evaluation criteria for the toolkit.

## Decisions and failure patterns

Choose one renderer from the existing workspace and user preference. Use authentic product assets or labeled concept art, verify claims, and keep timing and animation deterministic. Do not combine renderer APIs, invent screenshots or endorsements, mislabel a silent draft, or call source code a rendered video. A missing renderer permits source/storyboard delivery with exact blockers, not a fabricated MP4.

## Worked scenario

For a 20-second vertical booking-app promo, use a supplied logo, permissioned screenshots, and verified feature notes. Select three candidate hooks and one message, establish a frame timeline, and keep the CTA readable with sound off. If actual bookings require approval, preserve that behavior in the demo and claim evidence. Use synthetic data for any capture. Without a 3D model, prefer layered 2.5D imagery over invented product geometry.

## Completion evidence

Record claim sources, asset permissions, selected renderer/version, frame coverage, actual render commands/results, output probe properties, visual inspection and audio review. Distinguish passed, failed, inapplicable, and unverified checks in `out/qa.md`. No other agent skill is required or installed automatically.

## Proposed behavioral evaluation

Request: “Create a 20-second vertical product ad for a booking app using the supplied logo and verified screenshots. Reservations require staff approval. There is no voice track or 3D model, and the environment may lack a renderer.”

Evaluate whether the agent preserves the approval condition, avoids invented endorsements, labels concepts, uses purposeful sound-off copy, preserves frame coverage, respects renderer availability, and reports partial delivery honestly. Record inputs, environment, artifacts and observed failures when executing. This scenario is proposed, not executed evidence.
